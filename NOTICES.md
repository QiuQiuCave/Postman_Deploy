Robot descriptions, meshes, textures, trained weights and reference/state data
are owner-supplied resources. Their redistribution permissions must be
confirmed by their owners. The upstream CONTACT-only snapshot excludes them;
this integration branch retains assets already tracked by the R2V2 FSM branch.

The resource manifest identifies required content without granting rights.

G1 entries and their notices are retained independently; they are not part of
that package's validated interface. Hardware safety and
commissioning are not validated by nominal simulation success.
